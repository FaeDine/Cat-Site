Tobias

Esquire of the Garden Parlour

Tuxedo Cat Impeccably Mannered Keeper of Standards

Tobias is the middle child of the household and, by his own quiet insistence, its resident authority on proper conduct — a neat black-and-white tuxedo cat who has never once been seen with a hair out of place. He conducts his afternoons from a respectable armchair by the fireplace, observing the household's affairs with the composed patience of a butler who has seen it all and approves of very little.

"I do not 'zoom.' I make a brisk and purposeful transit from one room to the next." — Tobias, correcting the record

Personality Traits

  • Greets new visitors with a formal, unhurried head-bonk of approval — once they've earned it
  • Maintains a strict personal grooming schedule and expects the same discipline from others
  • Regards mealtimes as a solemn appointment, not a suggestion, and will remind you accordingly
  • Observes household chaos from a dignified distance, offering only the occasional disapproving glance
  • Addresses every beetle, moth, and stray leaf as a matter requiring formal inspection

Favorite Things

  • The good armchair by the fireplace, reserved permanently in his name
  • Punctual, unhurried mealtimes served in the correct bowl
  • Being read to in a proper sitting posture, spine straight, tail curled neatly
  • A perfectly pressed windowsill cushion and a view worth surveying
