Tobias

Count of the Candy Shoppe

Tuxedo Cat Impeccably Mannered Candy Connoisseur

Tobias is the middle child of the candy kingdom and, by his own quiet insistence, its resident authority on proper conduct — a neat black-and-white tuxedo cat who has never once been seen with a hair out of place. He conducts his afternoons from a respectable armchair beside the candy counter, observing the kingdom's affairs with the composed patience of a sommelier who has tasted it all and approves of very little.

"I do not 'pounce on gumdrops.' I conduct a refined tasting with meticulous appreciation." — Tobias, correcting the record

Personality Traits

  • Greets visitors to the candy shoppe with a formal, unhurried head-bonk of approval — once they've earned it
  • Maintains a strict personal grooming schedule and expects the same discipline from others
  • Regards candy tasting time as a solemn appointment, not a suggestion, and will remind you accordingly
  • Observes candy kingdom chaos from a dignified distance, offering only the occasional disapproving glance
  • Catalogues every variety of lollipop, gumball, and jellybean as a matter requiring formal inspection

Favorite Things

  • The good armchair by the candy counter, reserved permanently in his name
  • Punctual, unhurried candy tastings served in the correct bowl
  • Being read candies in a proper sitting posture, spine straight, tail curled neatly
  • A perfectly arranged selection of premium lollipops and a view worth surveying
