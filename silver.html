Silver

Guardian of the Gumdrop Grove

Silver-Grey Coat Elegant & Mysterious Secret Sweet Tooth

Silver is the youngest of the three and the hardest to pin down — a sleek, storm-grey cat who seems to appear and vanish among the tangle of gumdrop vines like a small ghost. By day she is cool, composed, and utterly uninterested in anyone's affection. By 3am she is a completely different creature entirely.

"I do not eat candy during daylight hours. Try again at 3am, when the gumdrops taste better." — Silver

Personality Traits

  • Vanishes into the gumdrop grove for hours, reappearing looking extremely pleased with herself
  • Runs full-speed laps around the candy kingdom at 3am for reasons known only to her
  • Pretends not to care about anyone, then falls asleep tucked under someone's chin
  • Has never once come when called, but always shows up the moment you sit down with candy
  • Moves in near-total silence — you simply turn around and she's just there with lollipop remnants on her whiskers

Favorite Things

  • The stack of candy boxes behind the gumdrop grove, for ambush purposes
  • Moonlight and the hour no sensible creature should be awake (perfect for raiding the stash)
  • The one specific candy dish, and only that candy dish
  • Watching cotton candy clouds with unsettling focus and barely concealed desire
