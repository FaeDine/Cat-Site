Silver

Midnight Steam Phantom

Silver-Grey Coat Elegant & Aloof Secret Cuddlebug

Silver is the youngest of the three and the hardest to pin down — a sleek, storm-grey cat who seems to appear and vanish among the tangle of pipework like a small ghost. By day she is cool, composed, and utterly uninterested in anyone's affection. By 3am she is a completely different creature entirely.

"I do not do affection during daylight hours. Try again at 3am, on my terms." — Silver

Personality Traits

  • Vanishes into the pipework for hours, reappearing looking extremely pleased with herself
  • Runs full-speed laps around the workshop at 3am for reasons known only to her
  • Pretends not to care about anyone, then falls asleep tucked under someone's chin
  • Has never once come when called, but always shows up the moment you sit down to relax
  • Moves in near-total silence — you simply turn around and she's just there

Favorite Things

  • The stack of crates behind the boiler house, for ambush purposes
  • Moonlight and the hour no sensible creature should be awake
  • The one specific blanket, and only that blanket
  • Watching pigeons through the brass porthole with unsettling focus
