Kaiser

Self-Appointed King of the Boiler Room

Orange Tabby Very Dramatic Professional Napper

Kaiser arrived at the workshop four summers ago as a scrawny stray who wandered in through the loading gate and simply never left — and within a week he had decided the entire place belonged to him. He rules from the warmest spot beside the boiler, surveys his kingdom (the scrap-metal yard, mostly) with a critical eye, and considers any closed door a personal insult.

"The food bowl is two hours from empty and yet here I sit, starving, wasting away." — Kaiser, every single morning

Personality Traits

  • Announces his opinions loudly and often, usually about dinner being late
  • Believes every lap is legally his the moment someone sits down
  • Stages elaborate protests (dramatic flops, disapproving stares) when denied treats
  • Guards the warmest patch of floor by the boiler like it's a throne
  • Surprisingly gentle with the workshop mice, terrifying to the mail carrier

Favorite Things

  • Warm laundry fresh off the radiator pipes
  • Supervising repairs from atop the workbench
  • Second breakfast (he insists this is a real meal)
  • The crinkle of the treat bag from three rooms away
