Kaiser

Self-Appointed King of the Windowsill

Orange Tabby Very Dramatic Professional Napper

Kaiser arrived at the cottage four summers ago as a scrawny stray who wandered in through the garden gate and simply never left — and within a week he had decided the entire place belonged to him. He rules from the sunniest windowsill, surveys his kingdom (the vegetable patch, mostly) with a critical eye, and considers any closed door a personal insult.

"The food bowl is two hours from empty and yet here I sit, starving, wasting away." — Kaiser, every single morning

Personality Traits

  • Announces his opinions loudly and often, usually about dinner being late
  • Believes every lap is legally his the moment someone sits down
  • Stages elaborate protests (dramatic flops, disapproving stares) when denied treats
  • Guards the sunniest patch of floor like it's a throne
  • Surprisingly gentle with the chickens, terrifying to the mail carrier

Favorite Things

  • Warm laundry fresh out of the dryer
  • Supervising garden chores from the fence post
  • Second breakfast (he insists this is a real meal)
  • The crinkle of the treat bag from three rooms away
