Kaiser

Lord of the Red Lollipop Realm

Red Lollipop Lover Very Dramatic Professional Napper

Kaiser arrived at the candy kingdom four summers ago as a scrawny stray who wandered in through the gumdrop gate and simply never left — and within a week he had decided the entire place belonged to him. He rules from the warmest spot beside the lollipop garden, surveys his kingdom (the licorice fields, mostly) with a critical eye, and considers any closed candy jar a personal insult.

"My candy stash is three lollipops from empty and yet here I sit, famished, wasting away." — Kaiser, every single morning

Personality Traits

  • Announces his opinions loudly and often, usually about candy being rationed
  • Believes every lap is legally his the moment someone sits down
  • Stages elaborate protests (dramatic flops, disapproving stares) when denied lollipops
  • Guards the warmest patch of floor by the lollipop garden like it's a throne
  • Surprisingly gentle with the candy birds, terrifying to the candy thief

Favorite Things

  • Warm caramel fresh from the candy kitchen
  • Supervising lollipop production from atop the candy counter
  • Second candy break (he insists this is a real time of day)
  • The crinkle of the candy wrapper from three rooms away
